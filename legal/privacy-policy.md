# Kitho Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Kitho ("we", "us") collects and uses your personal data when you use the Kitho app and website. It follows the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is **Kitho** (company number: [COMPANY NUMBER]; registered office: [REGISTERED ADDRESS]).

Contact for all privacy matters: **admin@kitho.co.uk**

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer. Use the email above for any request or question.

## 2. Who can use Kitho

Kitho is for adults only. You must be at least **18** years old to use Kitho. If we learn that a user is under 18, we delete their account and data.

## 3. What data we collect

**Data you give us**
- Name, email address, phone number, gender
- Photos, bio and profile details
- Messages you send in chats
- Reports and messages you send to us

**Data collected automatically**
- Location (only if you allow it in your device settings)
- Device data (device type, operating system, app version, identifiers)
- Usage data (how you use the app, log data, IP address)

**Data from third parties**
- If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and account identifier from them.

We do not knowingly collect special category data (such as sexual orientation, religion, ethnicity or health data). Please do not include it in your profile or bio. If you choose to share it, you do so voluntarily.

## 4. How and why we use your data

| Purpose | Data | Lawful basis (UK GDPR Art 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Create and manage your account, sign you in | Name, email, phone, sign-in data | Contract |
| Show your profile and let you chat with other users | Profile, photos, bio, messages | Contract |
| Show people near you | Location | Consent |
| Keep the service safe: moderation, reports, fraud and abuse prevention | Profile, messages, device and usage data | Legitimate interests (keeping users safe); legal obligation (Online Safety Act 2023) |
| Improve and fix the app | Device and usage data | Legitimate interests (improving our service) |
| Send marketing emails | Email | Consent |
| Send push notifications | Device identifier | Consent |
| Respond to your requests and complaints | Contact details, messages | Legitimate interests / legal obligation |

You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 9). Withdrawing does not affect earlier use.

## 5. Automated decisions

We do not use automated matching or profiling to decide who you are shown.

We use an automated reporting system for safety. Each report made against an account adds points to it. When the points reach a set limit, the account is automatically restricted. If more reports come in, the account is automatically banned. This can significantly affect your access to Kitho.

An administrator reviews every restriction and ban and can reverse it if it was a mistake. You can also ask for a human review, tell us your view or challenge a decision by messaging the administrator in the Inbox tab or emailing admin@kitho.co.uk.

## 6. Who we share your data with

- **Other users:** your profile, photos and messages are visible to other users as the app is designed to work.
- **Service providers (processors)** acting on our instructions:
  - Supabase (database, authentication and storage), region: Ireland
  - Render (hosting)
  - Google and Apple (sign-in)
- **Authorities:** the police, regulators (such as Ofcom or the ICO) or courts, when the law requires it or to protect users from serious harm.

We do not sell your personal data.

Google and Apple are also based in the United States. For these transfers we rely on UK-approved safeguards: the UK-US Data Bridge where the provider is certified, or otherwise the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in the provider's data processing terms. **[CONFIRM Render and Supabase transfer mechanism]**

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:
- **be informed** about how we use your data (this policy)
- **access** a copy of your data
- **correct** inaccurate data
- **erase** your data
- **restrict** how we use your data
- **data portability** (receive your data in a portable format)
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing
- **withdraw consent** at any time (in app settings, device settings or by emailing us)
- **not be subject to solely automated decisions** with legal or similarly significant effects

To use any right, email admin@kitho.co.uk. We reply within one month.
